01 — Metal Works

Decor Fabrication

We design and fabricate custom metal structures for homes, offices, factories, and commercial spaces. From ornate decorative gates to heavy industrial frameworks — done with precision.

  • MS & SS entrance gates, pedestrian gates, sliding gates
  • Compound walls, boundary fencing, security grills
  • Staircase railings (SS 304 / MS powder coated)
  • Window grills, ventilation covers, safety screens
  • Pergolas, canopies, shade structures
  • Decorative laser-cut panels and façade elements
  • Custom furniture frames and industrial racks
Free site visit — we come to you, measure, and give a detailed written quote at no charge.

03 — Water Solutions

Borewell Drilling

Professional borewell drilling using DTH and Rotary rigs. We handle everything — site assessment, drilling, casing, pump fitting, and yield testing. One team, complete solution.

  • Free site assessment & borewell feasibility check
  • DTH and Rotary drilling up to 600+ ft depth
  • PVC and MS casing installation
  • Submersible pump supply and installation (1HP–7.5HP)
  • Panel board, starter, and wiring
  • Yield testing and water quality report
  • Suitable for residential, commercial, agricultural use
Transparent drilling rate — quoted per foot with full material breakup. No surprises.

04 — Maintenance

Borewell Maintenance

Is your borewell giving low yield or your pump making noise? We diagnose and fix. Our maintenance team handles both emergency repairs and scheduled servicing.

  • Submersible pump removal, servicing & reinstallation
  • Borewell flushing and compressor air-cleaning
  • Low yield restoration and re-drilling advice
  • Pipe column replacement (GI / HDPE)
  • Starter panel and electrical troubleshooting
  • Annual maintenance contract (AMC) available
  • Emergency service — same day response
Same-day response for pump breakdowns. Call us and we'll send a technician ASAP.

Why Legends

Our Story
Built on Trust

Legends Infra was started with a simple mission — to deliver construction and fabrication services that truly last. We saw too many clients disappointed by poor quality work and hidden costs, and we decided to do things differently.

Over 8 years, we've grown from a small fabrication shop to a full-service infra company covering decor fabrication, interior fit-out, and borewell solutions across Chennai, Coimbatore, Trichy, Tirunelveli, and all of Tamil Nadu.

Every project gets the same treatment — personal attention, quality materials, honest pricing, and a team that shows up and gets the job done right.
