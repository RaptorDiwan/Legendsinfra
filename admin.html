Image Hosting Guide
HOW TO ADD PHOTOS
1

Take a photo of your work

Take a clear photo with your mobile. Good lighting works best. Save to your phone gallery.

2

Go to imgbb.com

This is a free photo hosting website. No account needed. Open it on your phone or computer.

3

Upload your photo

Click "Start Uploading" → choose your photo → click "Upload". It uploads in seconds.

4

Copy the "Direct Link"

After upload, you'll see links. Copy the one labeled "Direct link" — it ends with .jpg or .png

5

Paste in "Add New Post"

Go to the "Add New Post" tab, paste the link in the "Photo URL" box. The preview will show your image instantly.

Alternative: If you use Google Drive, upload the photo → right-click → "Get shareable link" → change the URL from drive.google.com/file/d/ID/view to drive.google.com/uc?id=ID. That gives a direct image link.
